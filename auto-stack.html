Potential Annual Benefit

Estimated savings using a 40,000 sq. ft. hangar, 5% utilization boost, $3.50/sq. ft. monthly tenant rate, 4 hours spent stacking per day, $35 per labor hour, and 3 crew members.

Auto-Stack: a 5% increase in hangar utilization represents a potential annual revenue increase of $84,000 under these assumptions.

Auto-Stack: potential annual labor savings of $7,665, equivalent to approximately 219 labor hours per year under these assumptions.

Tow playback: potential to reclaim ~876 labor hours per year (~2.4 labor hours per day), worth $30,660, spent stacking and unburying aircraft, under these assumptions.

Auto-Stack alone represents a potential $91,665 a year. Tow playback could also reduce new-hire training time with clear, repeatable tow guidance; the impact on training time has not yet been measured.
