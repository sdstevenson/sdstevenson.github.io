How We Calculate Your Savings

Our estimates are based on two measurable, real-world impact areas.

Potential Annual Revenue

Revenue = hangar area × utilization boost % × monthly tenant rate × 12.

Potential Labor Savings

The annual stacking labor baseline = crew count × hourly wage × stacking hours per day × 365. It is allocated by fixed shares: Auto-Stack 5%, Tow Paths 20%, Hangar Rash Prevention 30%, and Tug Automation 45%.

What the Estimate Excludes

System costs, incident cost avoidance, potential insurance savings, and value of staff reassignment are excluded. Actual results depend on your operation.
