HOW IT WORKS

Six steps, from the schedule to the record.

Four of them are running in hangars today. Two are being built now, on the same plan the first four produce — so nothing you adopt today gets thrown away later.

Running today
  1. 01
    Calendar view

    Know what the hangar needs next

    Arrivals and departures live in one shared calendar, imported from the tools you already run. No more three versions of today’s plan.

    Explore the calendar → Available now
  2. 02
    Auto-Stack

    Stop solving hangar tetris by hand

    Auto-Stack builds the layout in minutes, weighing departure times and the priority you assign each aircraft, then hands you a plan to review.

    Explore Auto-Stack → Available now
  3. 03
    Tow paths

    Settle the moves before the first tow

    Every aircraft gets a reviewed route and a place in the movement order, so the sequence is agreed on before anyone hooks up a tug.

    Explore tow paths → Available now
  4. 04
    Audit log

    Show exactly what happened, and when

    Movements are time-stamped as the day is worked, leaving a clean history you can hand to a customer, a tenant, or an insurer.

    Explore the audit log → Available now
Being built now
  1. 05
    Hangar rash prevention

    Catch a wingtip before it catches you

    Live aircraft tracking and proximity alerts while aircraft are in motion, with mixed and wing-over-wing stacking accounted for.

    Explore collision prevention → In development
  2. 06
    Tug integration

    Take the crew out of the tow loop

    Retrofit the tugs you already own and hand them an approved plan, so the same reviewed paths and clearances get driven without a wing-walker on every move.
