See exactly how to stack your hangar

Starlight builds your hangar layout in minutes, not hours, then shows exactly how to tow each aircraft into place. Layouts and tow paths are optimized to maximize hangar space and minimize time spent towing, placing aircraft leaving soon at the front of the hangar and aircraft staying for a while at the back.

  • Stacking plans in minutes
  • A reviewed tow path for every aircraft
  • Optimized for space and time
