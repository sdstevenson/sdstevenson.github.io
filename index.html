How the calendar works →How Auto-Stack works

See exactly how to stack your hangar

Starlight builds your hangar layout in minutes, not hours, then shows exactly how to tow each aircraft into place. Layouts and tow paths are optimized to maximize hangar space and minimize time spent towing, placing aircraft leaving soon at the front of the hangar and aircraft staying for a while at the back.

  • Stacking plans in minutes
  • A reviewed tow path for every aircraft
  • Optimized for space and time
How tow playback works →How Auto-Stack works

An extra set of eyes on every tow

In testing · Ready December 2026

As aircraft are towed into the hangar, Starlight tracks them and alerts the crew through hangar speakers and lights when clearances get too tight. You set the thresholds, so it only goes off when it matters.

  • Live aircraft tracking
  • Alerts only when needed
  • Speakers and lights in the hangar
  • Nothing mounted on the aircraft
How hangar rash prevention works →

Let autonomous tugs do the towing

In development · June 2027

Retrofit the tugs you already own and hand them the approved plan, so the same reviewed paths get driven fully autonomously. When your crew tows manually, an automatic brake stops the tug before the aircraft makes contact.

  • Fully autonomous moves from the same crew plan
  • Automatic braking during manual towing
  • Fewer people needed per move
How autonomous tugs work →

Replay any move, exactly as it happened

Starlight stores a playback of every aircraft move, alongside hangar video recorded when motion is detected or an aircraft is moved. If an incident happens, play it back and see exactly where each aircraft moved and how it moved. Close calls are tracked too, building a hangar safety score you can share with insurers and customers.

  • A playback of every move
  • Close calls tracked
  • A safety score to share

Give owners a live view of their aircraft

Aircraft owners can see where their aircraft is in your hangar, get notified when it moves, replay every past move, and see what’s planned next. Peace of mind for them, and one more reason to stay or sign with you.

  • Live location in the hangar
  • Notified when it moves
  • Past and planned moves
  • Only their own aircraft
