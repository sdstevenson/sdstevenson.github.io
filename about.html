Our Mission

Our mission is to make hangar operations easier to plan, coordinate, and carry out through connected hardware and software. We work directly with operators to understand their daily challenges and develop tools around how their hangars run.

Today, that means planning aircraft layouts and tow paths around flight schedules. We’re developing camera-based aircraft tracking to help reduce towing workload and collision risk. Our longer-term goal is autonomous aircraft movement: hardware and tug systems that carry out planned moves without human intervention.

Our Team
