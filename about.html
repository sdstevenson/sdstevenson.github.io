Our Mission

Starlight Hangars was founded to build a future where intelligent robotics make hangar operations safer and more efficient.

We believe that the tools powering aviation ground operations are long overdue for a transformation. By combining autonomy with deep FBO know-how, we are creating technology that works the way operators actually work.

Starlight Hangars is headquartered in the United States, with roots in the New England aviation community.
