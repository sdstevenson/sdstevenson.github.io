Tug Integration

We are developing tug automation to carry out approved aircraft moves and free your crew for higher-value work.

In development

From plan to movement

The goal: connect approved tow paths to automated aircraft movement.

Potential Annual Labor Savings: $68,985

Estimated ROI using a 40,000 sq. ft. hangar, 5% utilization boost, $3.50/sq. ft. monthly tenant rate, 4 hours spent stacking per day, $35 per labor hour, and 3 crew members.
