Capabilities in Testing

Multi-Sensor Array

Sensor placement will be tailored to your hangar layout to support aircraft tracking across monitored areas.

Real-Time Tracking

Alert System

Visual and audio alerts are being developed to draw operator attention to detected proximity risks.

Predictive Analysis

We are developing movement analysis to identify approaching aircraft conflicts and give crews more time to respond.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our hangar rash prevention system.

The system is in testing. Tracking accuracy and alert performance need to be validated for the aircraft, camera coverage, and operating conditions at each installation.
Visual and audio alerts are planned to help crews respond to detected risks. Automatic tug braking is a separate capability in development.
The system is being developed to support crews with aircraft tracking and proximity alerts. It does not currently replace wing walkers or your established towing procedures.
The planned sensor array will monitor aircraft positions within the configured coverage area. Sensor placement and coverage will be assessed for your hangar.
Installation planning will assess lighting, camera mounting locations, power, and network access for your hangar.
Costs will depend on your hangar layout, camera requirements, and installation needs. Contact us to discuss your site.
We are developing proximity checks around aircraft geometry, including wings, tail, nose, and fuselage. Detection performance will depend on coverage and validation for your aircraft.
Installation timing will depend on your site requirements and system readiness. Contact us to discuss a schedule for your hangar.
