Key Features

Record, replay, export, and retain every move in your hangar.

A playback of every move

Every aircraft move is stored as a playback showing exactly where each aircraft moved and how it moved.

Recorded on motion and aircraft moves

Video is recorded automatically when motion is detected or an aircraft is moved. You can also start a recording manually.

Easy event exports

Export recorded events to share with the people who need them.

User-chosen retention

Retain recorded events for as long as you choose.

Review from anywhere
